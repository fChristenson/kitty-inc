## Art direction for a selected batch

- Match the existing playful cartoon artwork: bold dark outlines, saturated colors, clear expressions, and simple shading.
- Use one dominant subject and at most two supporting props. The gag should still read at 250x250.
- Keep the entire subject in frame with breathing room around ears, feet, hats, and props.
- Use a plain near-white background, not a scene or a baked-in transparency checkerboard.
- Give pale clothing, glass, and white details closed dark outlines so background removal preserves them.
- Avoid tiny lettering, captions, logos, fine confetti, and scattered miniature coins. Add names and reward text in the game, not the artwork.
- Keep motion implied by the pose and a few broad shapes; avoid heavy blur or wispy trails that are difficult to cut out.
