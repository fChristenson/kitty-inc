## Gap analysis: what the 356 registered crits already cover

Saturated already — don't start another batch here without a strong angle:
office/corporate, savoury food, sweets, gaming culture, nerd-culture movie
references, dance styles, mythic creatures, grimdark space cats, Baldur's Gate,
Deus Ex, gems and valuables, gold and money, heavy-metal loot, earth metals,
superheroes, seasonal sales, poker hands, and historical character art.

Thin or missing — the five batches below fill the biggest holes. Space has only
four scattered entries (Space Race, Shooting Star, Cosmic Catapult, Orbital
Pounce), the sea has four (Booty, Fishing for Funds, Sea Monster Slayer, Coral
Sea Serpent), and sports, lab science and casino games have essentially nothing
despite fitting the game's money-and-luck framing perfectly.

Still open for later batches if these land well: construction and heavy
industry, vehicles and transport, non-metal music, garden and nature, detective
noir, circus and carnival, ancient Egypt, steampunk, farming, and library or
academia.
