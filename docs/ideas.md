# Ideas

## List thirty-seven

A floor crit that drinks and spits back, a flight that splits its target, and a lightning duel. Playground: `tmp/_playground/ideas.html?list=next34`.

- **Leech** (floor crit): the number shoots beams into every bar and drinks them, streams of sparks racing up the beams into it, faster and thicker, as it swells and shudders bigger and bigger; then it bursts, and the charge slams back down every beam at once, a rattle of blasts where each one meets its bar, the last on its own.

## List thirty-eight

A flight that breaks a planet, lightning that forks down the building, and a mist that lightning sets off. Playground: `tmp/_playground/ideas.html?list=next35`.

- **Planet buster** (flight event): the view closes on a planet of glitter; the guns carve a glowing X across its face, it cracks apart into a dozen chunks that tumble out past the view, every chunk shot down in a paying blast, then the bare core goes up in a huge one.

## List thirty-nine

Two looks at once in new pairs, like Ion haze: spray and galaxy, Shmup and lightning, drill and lightning. Playground: `tmp/_playground/ideas.html?list=next36`.

- **Arc raid** (Shmup + lightning event): the floors shrink away into space and a swarm of thirty drones hangs over the ship; the ship fires chain lightning that hops drone to drone, every hop a paying pop, each volley a longer chain, quicker and quicker, until the last leaps through every drone left and into the mothership behind them.
- **Bolt drill** (drill + lightning event): a bolt comes down out of the sky and grinds into the top bar like a drill bit, stalling and crackling in a gush of sparks, the bar jolting, then punches through in a blast and grinds into the next bar down, quicker each time, until it grounds out in a huge blast at the bottom.

## List forty-three

Not events: things the game doesn't have at all yet (it has no prestige, no gallery of the 1,000+ events, no way to share a crit, no phone buzz, no reason to look at the cats themselves). Text only; nothing gets built until one is picked.

- **Event gallery**: every animated crit you've seen unlocks a card in a gallery (the badge collection's twin, silhouettes for the unseen), sorted by look (Flight, Lightning, Galaxy...); tap a card to replay it, full screen, any time. Finishing a look's set gives a permanent income bonus, and the count (412 / 1,084) gives the game's huge event library a reason to chase.
- **Cat breeds**: every hired cat rolls a coat (tabby, black, white, calico, siamese, and rare ones: gold, rainbow, ghost) drawn by recolouring the same sprite; rare coats sparkle and earn more, managers can be any breed, and a breeds page shows which you've found. Gives the floors themselves something to look at and hope for on every hire.
- **Replay and share**: the game keeps the last few seconds of the screen; after a big crit a small "share" button saves it as a video clip (or GIF) with the company name and the number, ready to post.
- **Feel settings**: phones buzz in time with every blast and shake (navigator.vibrate, stronger on bigger hits), plus a settings page for music, sound, buzz and screen shake strength.

## List fifty

New event LOOKS, the way Shmup and Road race were: whole game genres played out by themselves in wisps, glitter, beams and blasts. Each would get its own shared lib and could carry many events, like the other looks. Playground: [`tmp/_playground/ideas.html?list=next41`](../tmp/_playground/ideas.html?list=next41).

- **Peggle**: a launcher wisp at the top aims and fires one ball into a field of forty glitter pegs; it ricochets down through them, lighting every peg it touches, and drops out the bottom; then every lit peg goes off in turn, quicker and quicker, levelling the bar behind it, the last in a huge blast.

## List fifty-eight

New concepts in the vein of the gestures list: the player's hands get a moment to act while a win is happening, and acting only ever adds to it (the base win is always paid). Text only; nothing gets built until one is picked.

- **Pump**: as a crit's number slams in, a gold gauge appears under it for a second. Tapping as fast as you can pumps it, each tap a click and a jolt, and the number grows from x5 towards x10 before it lands.
- **Catch**: after a big crit, coins rain down the screen. Drag the total readout like a basket to catch them, and every coin caught is added on top of the win.
- **Steer**: during an animated crit, dragging a finger bends the wisp's path towards it. Steered over extra bars it lands extra hits, so watching becomes playing.
