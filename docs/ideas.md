# Ideas

## List seventy-four (game features)

- **Payline**: every income bar on screen is a reel. Bars fill and pay at their own speeds, so they drift in and out of step. When all the on-screen bars happen to pay out at the same moment, that's a payline: the bars flash gold together and each one pays its payout ×7 at once. Boosts, upgrades and scrolling keep reshuffling the timing, so a line-up is never planned and always a surprise.
- **Twin floors**: when two neighbouring floors reach the very same level, they become twins. Their "Lvl" labels turn gold and a glittering link joins them. While they stay twins, every crit that lands on one also lands on the other. Buying either floor past the other breaks the link, and it re-forms whenever their levels meet again.
- **Lucky level**: any floor whose level ends in 7 (Lvl 7, 17, 107…) has a softly glowing gold label. A crit that lands on it goes up a tier, so x3 becomes x7 and x7 becomes x10. Lvl 77 and Lvl 777 make the next crit there certain. Buying levels turns into reading the labels for the next lucky one.

## List seventy-three (game features)

- **Hire pulls**: every hire is a gacha pull. The new cat drops in from a capsule with a rolled rarity (common, rare, epic, legendary), and the rarity multiplies that worker's income. Legendary cats glow and have their own walk. A plain buy becomes the most exciting button in the game, and a full floor is something to brag about.
- **Badge fusion**: badges you've collected can be fused. Pick any two and they melt into a new hybrid badge that has both rewards and its own sticker, made by laying the two stickers together. Which pairs make the rarest hybrids is a secret to discover. The badge collection becomes a crafting game with no cost, because the originals stay.
- **On beat**: the game gets a steady beat (music pulse, and the building's lights throb with it). Upgrade taps that land on the beat get a better crit roll, and a run of on-beat taps lifts it further. Off-beat taps are never worse than now. Tapping becomes a rhythm game riding on the slot machine.
