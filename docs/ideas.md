# Ideas

## List eighty-two

Floor crits, playable in the playground: [ideas.html?list=next62](../tmp/_playground/ideas.html?list=next62).

- **Blink**: the number vanishes in a flash and reappears by a bar, striking it, then blinks away again and again, quicker and quicker, all over the building, till it's everywhere at once, a copy by every bar, trembling; then every copy strikes together and the bars go off in a barrage, its own bar's the biggest.
- **Shield break**: a dome of light closes over the building and the number hammers it from outside, each hit splitting cracks across it, quicker and harder; then the dome shatters and its shards rain down onto every bar in a rattling barrage, its own bar's the biggest.

## List eighty-one

Floor crits, playable in the playground: [ideas.html?list=next61](../tmp/_playground/ideas.html?list=next61).

- **Gravity flip**: the number shoots up to the top of the screen and gravity flips: every bar falls up off its floor, faster and faster, slamming into the top one after another and piling up there, rattling; then gravity flips back and they all drop, crashing onto their floors in rattles of blasts, its own bar falling farthest and landing last and hardest.
- **Comet**: the number comes back as a comet tearing in from the top corner and swinging down across the building in a long curve, its glitter tail sweeping over every bar, each flaring in a rattle of blasts as the head crosses it; it loops back round, quickening, and nose-dives into its own bar in a huge blast.
- **Line clear**: the number shatters into blocks of light that drop onto every bar, filling its row cell by cell, faster and faster, each landing with a clack; when every row is full they flash and clear one after another from the top in runs of blasts, its own bar's row last and biggest.
- **Stampede**: the number bursts into a herd of wisps that gallop in from the left along every bar and ram its end, shoving every bar along its floor in a skid of sparks till they're crushed against the far wall; then the herd bursts and the bars spring back one after another in rattles of blasts, its own bar last.

## List eighty

Floor crits, playable in the playground: [ideas.html?list=next60](../tmp/_playground/ideas.html?list=next60). Each builds up over the whole building, faster and faster, then lets go.

- **Ball storm**: the number drops as a ball of light that bounces off a bar and splits in two, each bouncing off another bar and splitting again, doubling and quickening until dozens of balls ricochet round the building in a rattle of little blasts; they freeze for a heartbeat, then all go off at once and the bars blow one after another from the top, its own bar last.
- **Sonar**: the number rises into a wisp over the building that sends pings down through it, rings of light sweeping over every bar and lighting each up; the pings come quicker and quicker until they lock on, then one huge pulse thunders down the building and every bar blows as it hits, its own bar last with a huge blast.
- **Popcorn**: the bars all start to sizzle, glitter kernels lining their tops; one pops, then another, then more and more, faster and faster, every pop a little blast and a puff of glitter, till they're going off everywhere at once, and the last of them blow every bar in a barrage, its own bar's the biggest.
- **Tide**: a tide of glitter rises up the building from the street, swallowing the bars one by one, each lighting up as it goes under, rising faster and faster; over the top bar it boils and churns, then erupts: every bar blows at once in a barrage racing out from its middle, its own bar's the biggest.

## List seventy-nine

Floor crits, playable in the playground: [ideas.html?list=next59](../tmp/_playground/ideas.html?list=next59). Each builds up in every bar at once, then lets go.

- **Lattice**: the number zips round the building dropping a node of light on both ends of every bar, and beams snap on between them, bar to bar, until a web of light laces the building; it hums, brightening and thickening, then every node implodes into its bar and blasts race in from both ends of every bar to meet in its middle, its own bar's the biggest.
- **Collider**: a ring of light opens round the whole building and the number splits into two particles racing round it opposite ways, faster and faster, flashing past each other, their trails stretching into streaks; they smash together under the ground bar and the debris showers up onto every bar in a rattling barrage.
- **Constellation**: the number bursts into stars that scatter onto every bar, and beams join them one by one into a figure zigzagging down the building; it blazes brighter, then a spark races along it and every star goes nova as it arrives, star by star down the building, its own bar's last star the biggest.
- **Magnet**: every bar turns into a magnet, glitter streaming along loops of field from its right end round to its left, above and below; the flow quickens and the loops pull in tighter and brighter, then the poles flip: the loops whip out wide and the bars blow one after another from the top, its own bar last with a huge blast.

## List seventy-eight

Floor crits, playable in the playground: [ideas.html?list=next58](../tmp/_playground/ideas.html?list=next58). Each builds tension in every bar at once, then lets it all go.

- **Time bombs**: the number bursts into bomb wisps that stick themselves along every bar, fuses lit; they all blink together, faster and faster, the ticking rattling the building harder, then at zero they go off bar by bar from the top in a rattling chain, its own bar last with a huge blast.

## List seventy-seven

Floor crits, playable in the playground: [ideas.html?list=next57](../tmp/_playground/ideas.html?list=next57).

- **Whipcrack**: the number grabs the top bar's left end and cracks it like a whip: a ripple races along the bar, growing as it goes, and its tip snaps with a blast and a spray of glitter; it hops down and cracks each bar quicker, then cracks every bar at once, their tips going off in a barrage, its own bar's the biggest.
- **Zipper**: the number zips along the top bar, splitting it open behind it with light pouring out of the seam; at the end the bar snaps shut from end to end in a run of blasts, and the number hops down to zip the next one, quicker and wider, its own bar's seam the widest and its snap ending in a huge blast.
- **Hinge**: the number hops down the bars' right ends knocking each one loose, so it drops and hangs from its left end, swinging and creaking; then it comes back over the top and slams them back up into place one by one, each in a rattle of blasts, its own bar last with a huge blast.
- **Trampoline**: the number drops onto the middle of the top bar, which sags under it and springs it up and over onto the next bar down, each sagging deeper and flinging it higher; its own bar sags deepest and trembles, then fires it straight up through every bar above, each flexing up with a blast, into a huge blast over the top.

## List seventy-six

Floor crits, playable in the playground: [ideas.html?list=next56](../tmp/_playground/ideas.html?list=next56).

- **Equalizer**: the number dives into a speaker wisp thumping to a quickening beat, every bar's fill jumping with it like a level meter, glitter popping off each tip; a beat of silence drops them all empty, then on the drop they slam full and blasts race along every bar into a huge one on the ground bar's end.
- **Rubber band**: the number grabs the top bar's right end and hauls it out across the screen, stretching it like elastic, then lets go: it snaps back with a blast and quivers, flinging the number down onto the next bar, stretched farther and quicker; the ground bar's snap goes off in a huge blast and a barrage up every bar's end.
- **Reels**: every bar turns into a slot reel, coins and copies of the number streaking sideways through it; they stop one by one from the top with a clunk, a bounce and a blast, the number in every window, each wait longer; when the ground bar stops they're all lined up and a jackpot barrage zigzags down every bar into one huge blast.

## List seventy-five

Floor crits, playable in the playground: [ideas.html?list=next55](../tmp/_playground/ideas.html?list=next55).

- **Overflow**: the number dives into the top bar, which fills past full, bulges white-hot and bursts out of one end in a rattle of blasts and a gush of glitter arcing down onto the bar below; that one fills quicker and bursts out of its other end, on down, the ground bar bursting from both ends in a barrage closing on one huge blast.
- **Pincer**: the number splits in two and the halves fly out to the screen's sides, then slam back in along the top bar from both ends, clapping in its middle in blasts that squeeze the bar and squirt glitter; out and in again on each bar down, quicker each time; then eight copies clap every bar at once and blasts race out from every middle to the ends.
- **Hornets**: the number bursts into a buzzing swarm of tiny wisps that boils round the building, dive-bombs the top bar in a rattling string of little stings, lifts off angrier and quicker onto each bar down, then pours back into the number, which slams the ground bar in a huge blast and a barrage.

## Built

- **Meltdown** (`meltdownCrit` floor crit): bars heat white-hot together via `heatIncomeBar`, then blow top to bottom in runs of blasts, its own bar last.
- **Crunch** (`crunchCrit` floor crit): a gravity well hauls every bar into a stack (drawn by the crit through `drawBar`/`hideBars`), squeezes it, then flings them back to land in blasts, its own bar last.
- **Atoms** (`atomsCrit`), **Reactor** (`reactorCrit`), **Static** (`staticCrit`) floor crits: electron orbits, a core beaming into every bar, and static arcs, each building in every bar at once before going off.
- **EMP** (`empCrit`, bars shorted via `shortIncomeBar`) and **Big Bang** (`bigBangCrit`, the world and HUD zoomed via `shared/screenZoom`) floor crits.
