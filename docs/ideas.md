# Ideas

## List seventy-three (game features)

- **Hire pulls**: every hire is a gacha pull. The new cat drops in from a capsule with a rolled rarity (common, rare, epic, legendary), and the rarity multiplies that worker's income. Legendary cats glow and have their own walk. A plain buy becomes the most exciting button in the game, and a full floor is something to brag about.
- **Badge fusion**: badges you've collected can be fused. Pick any two and they melt into a new hybrid badge that has both rewards and its own sticker, made by laying the two stickers together. Which pairs make the rarest hybrids is a secret to discover. The badge collection becomes a crafting game with no cost, because the originals stay.
- **On beat**: the game gets a steady beat (music pulse, and the building's lights throb with it). Upgrade taps that land on the beat get a better crit roll, and a run of on-beat taps lifts it further. Off-beat taps are never worse than now. Tapping becomes a rhythm game riding on the slot machine.
