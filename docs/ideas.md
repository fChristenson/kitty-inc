## List 113 ([playground](../tmp/_playground/ideas.html?list=next91))

Floor crits.

- **Yo-yo**: the number drops off a glowing hand over the roof on a string of light, slams into a bar in a blast and zips back up; the hand moves over and throws it onto a lower bar, quicker each time. Then it whips once round the hand and fires straight down into its own bar in a huge blast.
- **Harpoons**: from the screen's side the number fires harpoons quick-fire into the bars, each trailing a line of light and thunking in with a blast. It yanks every line at once, blasts racing back along them, and reels itself in onto its own bar.
- **Bolas**: from the top corner the number flings bolas, two wisps on a short line whirling end over end; each wraps round its bar, winding tighter, and blows in a pair of blasts. The last, a fat one, wraps its own bar.
- **Javelins**: the number hurls javelins of light in high arcs; each sticks point-first in a bar, quivering, and blows a beat later, so the blasts chase the throws. A giant one skewers its own bar, shudders and goes off.
