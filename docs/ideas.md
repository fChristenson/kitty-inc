# Ideas

## List 117: game features ([playground](../tmp/_playground/ideas.html?list=next101))

Small surprise moments in what's already on screen (the total, the buttons, the crit numbers, the cats), in the spirit of the Odometer and the crit chains.

1. **Mystery price**: now and then a floor's button shows `???` instead of its price; tapping it reveals what the level cost: the normal price, half, nothing at all, or nothing plus ten levels on top.
2. **Loose digit**: a digit in the total sometimes wobbles loose and glows; tap it and it pops out as a coin, its value (a 9 most of all) paid into the total before it snaps back in.
3. **Catch the number**: a crit number sometimes hangs a beat longer with a gold rim; tap it then and it jumps a tier (x3 to x10, x10 to x50) before it lands.
4. **Lasso**: a ring of coins spins over a floor; draw a loop round it with a finger and the whole ring is caught and pulled into the total.
5. **High five**: two cats walking past each other sometimes stop and high-five, a coin popping up between them; tap it and both cats light up with a boost.

## List 118: game features

More small moments in the bars, buttons, scrolling and total, each a surprise that only pays.

1. **Golden lap**: now and then a bar's fill runs gold for one lap, glinting as it climbs; when it pays, that payout is ten times the normal one and slams into the total.
2. **Hot hand**: tapping a floor's button fast catches it alight, the flame growing with every quick tap; a run of twenty without a pause makes the next tap a sure crit.
3. **Roof bounce**: fling the building hard up to the top and it overshoots, the roof bouncing off the sky and shaking loose a spray of coins into the total.
4. **Rebound**: one gold coin in a stream flying into the total sometimes bounces off it and drops back onto a floor's bar, filling it at once.
5. **Hundreds**: a floor reaching a round hundred levels (Lvl 100, 200, ...) has its label slam gold and its bar pay out once for every hundred it's reached.

## List 119: game features

Still small moments in what's on screen, each a little surprise that only pays.

1. **Keep the change**: a buy now and then clinks, and part of its price (a tenth to a half) rattles back out of the button as coins into the total.
2. **Conga**: boosting every cat on a floor at once sometimes sets them dancing in a conga line across the floor, a coin popping off each one as it passes the bar.
3. **Change of tier**: a floor climbing a crit tier has its bar's new colour pour in from the left like paint, a coin popping out at the end.

## List 120: game features

More little surprises in what's already on screen, each one only paying.

1. **Domino**: a floor's payout now and then knocks the floor above into paying early, and it climbs the building floor by floor, each bar jolting as it pays, the last one slamming.
2. **Price drop**: a floor's price sometimes rolls down digit by digit like an odometer running backwards, holding the low price for a few seconds before it rolls back up.
3. **Cat nap**: a cat sometimes nods off at its desk without slowing anything, a little z floating up; tap it and it jolts awake and works at double speed for a while.
4. **Echo**: a crit number sometimes echoes, a smaller copy of it landing on the floor above a beat later with its own levels.

## List 140: game features ([playground](../tmp/_playground/ideas.html?list=next117))

New things the game doesn't have yet, not more of the same crits.

1. **Cat crits** (a new special group beside floor and income crits): a crit can land on the cats instead of the bars: the number bursts into wisps that zip to every cat in view one after another, each cat jumping as it climbs a perma tier in a pop and stays lit; the crit's own floor's cats last, all at once, in the huge blast.
2. **Jackpot lever** (a new boost beside Sale and Overtime): a floor's button turns into a gold Spin button for fifteen seconds; every tap spins a reel of crit numbers in it that rattles to a stop, the number flying onto the bar as its levels; three of the same in a row and the button hits the jackpot: every bar in view blows.
3. **Golden hour** (a new boost): the whole building turns gold, bar by bar from the bottom; for twenty seconds every bar pays on its own beat, coins pouring up into the total, the beats quickening as the hour runs out, until every bar pays at once and the total slams.

## List 142: crit groups rolled on other events ([playground](../tmp/_playground/ideas.html?list=next119))

Special groups that roll on something other than buying a level (today every floor, income, badge and animated crit comes off a crit on an upgrade tap).

1. **Unlock crits** (rolled on buying a floor): the lock bursts open, a crit number pops over the new floor and bursts into wisps that dive onto its bar in quick fire, the last huge. The new floor starts with levels.
2. **Payout crits** (rolled on a bar paying out): the full bar's payout catches a number that slams onto its end; the bar heats and gushes a geyser of coins into the total, which slams. The payout multiplied.
3. **Hire crits** (rolled on hiring a worker): the new cat lands glowing gold and wisps zip out of it to every empty desk in view, a cat dropping onto each with a blast; then every cat jumps. Free hires.
4. **Boost crits** (rolled on starting a Sale or Overtime): beams jump from the boosted button to every other button in view, each turning into a Sale with a blast, its bar glowing hot; then they all flash at once. The boost on every floor in view.

## List 143: more crit groups rolled on other events ([playground](../tmp/_playground/ideas.html?list=next120))

More special groups off triggers that roll nothing today.

1. **Tap crits** (rolled on tapping a cat): the tapped cat jumps and a crit number pops over it, then bursts into a wisp that hops cat to cat across the building in quick fire, each cat jumping lit as it's boosted, the last in the huge blast. Every cat in view boosted.
2. **Manager crits** (rolled when a manager's own boost goes off, with no tap at all): the manager's pulse turns into a shockwave that rolls out over the whole building, every bar it crosses jolting with levels in a rolling run of blasts; then it snaps back onto the manager's bar. Levels on every bar in view.
3. **Gauge crits** (rolled when an Overtime gauge fills): the full gauge overflows, gold pouring off its end in wisp streams onto every other bar, each turning gold from its left end with a blast; then they all blow at once. A crit tier on every bar in view.
4. **Welcome-back crits** (rolled on opening the game after time away): the money earned while away lands in the middle, a crit number slams beside it and it flips over onto the bigger sum, then bursts into a river of coins into the total, which slams. The time-away money multiplied.

## List 144: crit groups rolled on other events ([playground](../tmp/_playground/ideas.html?list=next121))

Three more triggers that roll nothing today.

1. **Max crits** (rolled when a floor reaches the level cap): the bar turns gold and MAX slams on it; then it shatters into gold shards that rain onto every other bar in quick fire. The levels it can't take any more go to the others.
2. **Spawn crits** (rolled on catching a random spawn: the mouse, a bubble, the wisp's glitter): the popped bubble's crit number splits into new bubbles that float off onto every bar, each bursting on it with its levels, quicker each, the last huge.
3. **Streak crits** (rolled when three crits land in a row in one hold): the three numbers stack up over the button, then fly together and fuse into their sum, which slams onto the bar in a run of blasts along it. The three crits paid again as one.

## List 196: new animation styles ([playground](../tmp/_playground/ideas.html?list=next173))

New animation styles, each with its own verb and lib in `shared/animationStyles`. They are aimed at the gaps in `node scripts/crit-dist.mjs`: rate crits have only three crits; floor crits lean on explosions. Each cell shows one crit from a thin group.

1. **Wind** (gusts; `shared/animationStyles/wind`: streaks of wind racing along things with glitter riding them, ending in a gale). Shown as **Tailwind**, a rate crit (rate crits have three):
   - Gusts streak along every bar from its left end, quicker each time.
   - Each gust shoves the bar on and turns its readout over a step faster.
   - The last gust is a gale, and its own bar ends twice as fast.
2. **Flip** (turning over; `shared/animationStyles/flip`: tiles turning over in a running wave to a gold face, quicker and quicker, like dominoes). Shown as **Flip Over**, a floor crit (floor crits are a fifth explosion):
   - A wisp hops along each bar, and every tile it lands on flips gold.
   - Each finished bar gives its levels.
   - On its own bar, every tile flips at once from the middle out, into a huge blast.

## List 201: crits for the thin spots ([playground](../tmp/_playground/ideas.html?list=next178))

Each idea fills a gap in `node scripts/crit-dist.mjs`.

1. **Housewarming** (lock, Snowball; lock has no snowball): a ball rolls across the dark locked floor, gathering its dust, bigger and quicker each pass. It bursts the floor open (free floor), and the new bar's first payouts pay double.
2. **Money Circle** (income, Summon; income has no summon): a magic circle is drawn on the roof under the readout. It turns faster and faster, paying a share every turn, then a pillar of light erupts through the readout.
3. **Paint Job** (floor, Spray; floor has no spray): a nozzle wisp glides down the side of the building, faster and faster, hissing gold mist along every bar it passes until its coat flashes with levels; then it swoops onto its own bar for a heavy coat and a huge blast.
4. **Cut Loose** (animated, Slice, free floor; animated has no slice): slashes cut across the dark locked floor in quick fire, glitter spilling out. A final cross tears it open, unlocked free.

## List 202: drawn styles, all drama ([playground](../tmp/_playground/ideas.html?list=next179))

Groups and styles drawn by `scripts/select-animation-style.mjs`. Each one builds up and ends in a climax.

1. **Crosshairs** (rate, Beam): a turret wisp at its own bar's end scans an aim laser up and down the building, faster and faster, locking a reticle onto every readout. It charges, trembling and glowing, then fires every beam at once: each bar's next payout pays five times, its own ten.
2. **Big Crunch** (income, Galaxy): a tilted disk of hundreds of stars spins up under the total, faster and faster and shrinking, a flare and a share each time. It crunches to a point and a jet of light shoots up into the readout.
3. **Deep Bore** (button, Drill): drills bite under every button at once and stall in a rumble, then bore in like pistons round the buttons, faster and faster. They punch through together: each button's taps also level the floor above.
5. **Rebound** (lock, Bounce): a wisp ricochets round the dark locked floor, faster each hit, splitting into two, then four. They all slam into its middle and burst it open: the new bar runs twice as fast for 30 seconds.
6. **Drain** (floor, Pull): its own bar's readout opens into a hole that sucks the fill out of every other bar, the green tearing loose as glitter and spiralling in, a trickle then a flood; wisps breaking away are yanked back. The hole swells, swallows the last, collapses to a point and bursts, wisps flying back onto every bar with levels.
7. **Boss Rush** (animated, Shmup, crit tiers): a giant boss of three wisp rings, each firing its own pattern while the ship peels it off in a rolling chain. The bare core charges and fires a beam the ship dodges, then the ship's beam blows it apart: every bar a crit tier up.
8. **Thread The Needle** (floor, Portal): rings stand at both ends of every bar. A wisp threads the building, out of each bar's right ring into the next bar's left, round and round, faster each time, levels on every run. It loops its own bar three times flat out, then bursts.

## List 203: the Crush style ([playground](../tmp/_playground/ideas.html?list=next180))

One element crushing another (`shared/animationStyles/crush`), shown three ways.

1. **Stamp Down** (floor, down; was Pile Driver, a name the Piledriver event has): a slab of light slams down on a heap of coins on its own bar, quicker and deeper each, glitter squeezed out, cracks spreading. The last slam crushes it flat, glitter jetting along the bar, the shock landing levels on every bar.
2. **Vice** (lock, sideways): two slabs close in on a big wisp in the dark locked floor like a vice, squeezing it thinner each slam. The last crushes it to a sliver and it bursts, the floor opening.
3. **Hard Sell** (income, the amount as the crusher): the green amount slams down on a big wisp under the readout like a press, a share each slam. The last crushes it flat and the amount shoots up into the readout.
4. **Hydraulic** (floor, a press): a slab of light lowers onto stacks of coins on its own bar and pushes without letting up, trembling harder, glitter oozing out, the stacks giving in jolts closer and closer, levels each, until they crumble into tumbling coins and dust.
