# Ideas

## List sixty-two

One idea of each kind. [Playground](../tmp/_playground/ideas.html?list=next45) for the animated one.

- **Topple** (floor crit): the number lands on the top bar and copies of it drop onto it one by one, a thud each, stacking into a tall wobbling tower. It tips over and the copies rain off it top first, each slamming onto a bar below for its levels, the last onto the bottom bar the biggest.
- **Wall stickers** (badge crits): a badge that lands sticks its sticker on that floor's wall, glinting, up to three per floor. Each sticker adds a little to the floor's income for good; a new one replaces the oldest, so the building slowly wears your luck.
- **Golden floor** (game feature): one random floor of the building is golden, its bar and button trimmed gold. Its crits land a tier higher. When it maxes out, the gold hops to another random floor with a flourish.
