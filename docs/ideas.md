# Ideas

## List twenty-four

Made of light and cluster blasts, two flights and two floor crits. Playground: `tmp/_playground/ideas.html?list=next21`.

- **Spiral turret** (floor crit): the number turns into a turret wisp spinning faster and faster between the bars, spraying a two-armed spiral of bullet wisps that blast every bar they cross in a rattling stream all over the building, then a full ring of bullets at once and a huge blast on its own bar.
- **Shockwave** (floor crit): the number slams down onto its bar and three shockwave rings of glitter roll out of the impact one after another; wherever a ring sweeps across a bar, a chain of blasts races along it from the middle out to both ends, then a huge blast back on its own bar.

## List twenty-seven

More flight events, all glitter, wisps, beams and cluster blasts. Playground: `tmp/_playground/ideas.html?list=next24`.

- **Spin station:** a huge wheel of wisps turning on beam spokes looms in out of the distance; the guns blow its rim wisps one after another as they wheel past, each spoke snapping as its wisp goes, then the hub goes up in a huge blast.
- **Wall breach:** a wall of wisps in rows fills the view as it rushes in, and the guns rake it row by row like a typewriter, every wisp bursting in turn in a long rattling string of blasts, then the view bursts through the hole.
- **Black hole:** the view dives at a black hole ringed by a whirling disk of glitter; wisps spiral in on tightening orbits, faster and faster, each bursting in a blast as it hits the rim, then the hole collapses in a huge flash.
- **Pulsar:** the view flies at a pulsar sweeping twin beams round like a lighthouse, faster and faster; every satellite wisp orbiting it blows as a beam sweeps across it, whole lines at a time in rattling clusters, then the pulsar flares in a huge blast.
