Pity/anticipation meter — a small visible (or semi-hidden) "luck" bar that fills toward the next crit, so players feel a win coming due even though the roll is still independent. Gacha games use this heavily to sustain engagement between real wins.

Golden-cookie-style random bonus event — a glowing icon spawns at a random spot/time on screen (independent of any click), and tapping it before it disappears grants an outsized reward (temp income multiplier, free upgrades, or a cash windfall). This is the textbook idle-game engagement driver (Cookie Clicker's golden cookie) — pure variable-ratio reinforcement, and distinct from your existing crit system because it doesn't require clicking a floor to trigger it, so it pulls attention back to an idle tab.


Combo/heat meter on rapid clicking — decays if you stop, rewards bursts of engagement, visually escalates (screen color grade, particle density) the hotter it gets.
