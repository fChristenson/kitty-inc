# Ideas

## Every purchase is a pull

- **Rarity rolls on floors:** each unlocked floor rolls common, rare or legendary. A rare floor gets its own look and a built-in multiplier, so every unlock is a spin.
- **Rarity rolls on hires:** a new cat can hatch as a rare variant that already has a perma tier. Hiring stops being a chore and becomes a lever.
- **Golden buildings:** a newly bought building has a small chance to come out gold, with a big reveal on the map and a permanent income bonus.
- **Spend meter:** every purchase fills a meter in the HUD; when it's full it fires an event. Surplus cash turns into thrills instead of piling up.

## Things worth saving up for

- **The lever:** a slot lever in the HUD that costs a few minutes of nominal income per pull and always lands an event or a featured crit. The price climbs with each pull and resets per building.
- **Progressive jackpot:** a slice of all spending feeds a jackpot pot that keeps growing on screen. Any crit has a tiny chance to hit it, paying out the whole pot in a giant event.
- **Wonders:** one-off landmarks on the map (a statue, a casino tower, a fountain) that cost hours of income. Each adds a permanent new effect, such as an extra event roll or a higher featured-crit chance.
- **Mystery capsules:** buy a capsule that hatches into a random badge you haven't found yet, with a gacha-style reveal. It gives late-game cash a use and fills the collection.

## New stages for events

- **Map events:** events that play out on the city map, hitting several buildings at once with free floors, hires or tiers.
- **Cross-building events:** an event that pans the camera from one building to the next, rewarding each one along the way.
- **Lucky floor:** one floor per building glows each session; crits there land one tier higher.

## Collection

- **Foil moment:** when a badge hits 10, 100 or 1000 during play, its sticker flips over in the game with the foil reveal, not only in the dialog.
- **Posters on the walls:** foil badges you own hang as posters in the floors' rooms, so the collection shows in the world.
- **Prismatic tier:** at 1000 copies the sticker gets a full holo treatment and its reward upgrades one step.

## Feel

- **Hold music that builds:** music layers stack up the longer a hold lasts and hit a drop when a big crit lands.
