# Ideas

## Progression and long-term goals

- **Prestige layer:** sell a whole company for a permanent multiplier or a new currency, giving the late game a reset loop.
- **Milestone goals per building:** short targets ("unlock 5 floors", "land 10 crits here") that each pay out a reward event.
- **Daily streak and featured category:** for example, food crits are twice as likely today.
- **Collection set bonuses:** completing a crit category in the Special Crits dialog grants a permanent boost.
- **Event codex:** track which events you've seen, with a "new event!" tag the first time each one plays.
- **Category mastery:** a progress bar per main group (girls, food, misc, wealth) toward a permanent perk.
- **Featured crit duplicates level up:** every tenth copy upgrades its reward and gives its sticker a gold rim.

## The slot-machine feeling

- **Near-miss and anticipation beats:** briefly tease a rarer tier before the crit resolves.
- **Combo meter during holds:** crits landing close together build a multiplier that pays out when the hold ends.
- **Jackpot pity:** guarantee a featured or ultra crit after N rolls without one.
- **Rarity reveal for featured crits:** a colour or frame shows how rare a crit is before its icon appears.
- **Hold rhythm bonus:** releasing and pressing again on a beat briefly raises crit odds.
- **Crit chains on screen:** crits landing on several floors within a second link up with lightning and pay a chain bonus.
- **Golden worker:** a rare cat walks across a floor; tapping it before it leaves triggers an event right away.
- **Dynamic event odds:** the event chance rises a little the longer it has been since the last event.

## Clarity and onboarding

- **First-session tutorial:** tap, hold, hire, unlock a floor, open the map.
- **"Best next buy" hint:** highlight the purchase with the best payback.
- **Income breakdown panel:** where income comes from (floors, boosts, perma tiers, global boosts).
- **Offline earnings choice:** the "You have earned" overlay offers an event or a ×2.

## Content

- **Building themes that change gameplay:** for example, a casino building with more crits, or a bakery with more food crits.
- **Investor visit events:** rare, longer events with a big payout at the end.
- **Seasonal rotations:** tie the odds of seasonal crits to the real calendar.
- **Floor synergies:** neighbouring floors at the same perma tier link up for a bonus.
- **Rival companies:** AI companies on the map grow over time; overtaking one unlocks a merger bonus.
- **District bonuses:** neighbouring buildings on the map share a small income boost.
- **Company traits:** each new company rolls a trait (faster workers, cheaper floors, more events).
- **Catch-up boost after idle:** coming back after hours gives a short rush with more crits.
- **Smarter map long-press buyer:** pick a strategy (cheapest first, best payback first, keep a reserve).

## Feel and polish

- **Photo mode:** hide the HUD and freeze the frame mid-event so the moment can be shared.
- **"Biggest hit" log:** a panel of your top crits and events.
- **Event replays:** watch a seen event again from the codex.
- **Sticker album:** the Special Crits dialog laid out as pages per category, with a fanfare when a page is complete.

## Developer workflow

- **Event gallery page:** play every event in a grid or one after another, to spot look-alikes.
- **Automatic perf check before deploy:** run the perf rig at 4x CPU and fail when frame times are worse than the baseline.
- **Real-device perf reports:** upload perf rig results from phones.
- **Cloud save or export code:** saves currently live only in localStorage.
