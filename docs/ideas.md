# Ideas

## List twenty-four

Made of light and cluster blasts, two flights and two floor crits. Playground: `tmp/_playground/ideas.html?list=next21`.

- **Spiral turret** (floor crit): the number turns into a turret wisp spinning faster and faster between the bars, spraying a two-armed spiral of bullet wisps that blast every bar they cross in a rattling stream all over the building, then a full ring of bullets at once and a huge blast on its own bar.
- **Shockwave** (floor crit): the number slams down onto its bar and three shockwave rings of glitter roll out of the impact one after another; wherever a ring sweeps across a bar, a chain of blasts races along it from the middle out to both ends, then a huge blast back on its own bar.

## List twenty-eight

Back on the building, two floor crits and two events, all wisps, bolts, glitter and cluster blasts. Playground: `tmp/_playground/ideas.html?list=next25`.

- **Shooting stars** (floor crit): the number bursts into five shooting stars that ricochet off the screen's sides; every bar one crosses blasts and splits it into two smaller stars, cascading from five to ten to twenty in a rattling storm of hits all over the building.
- **Popcorn** (floor crit): every bar heats up glowing and starts popping, little bursts flinging glitter kernels off its top, faster and faster like popcorn in a pan, until each bar bursts in turn in a big blast.
- **Lightning ladder** (lightning event): a bolt cracks up out of the ground onto the bottom bar and fans out along it in a rattle of strikes, then leaps up to the next bar and the next, quicker each time, until bolts crack down out of the sky onto every bar at once.
